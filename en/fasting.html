21 days of prayer and fasting

We begin the new year by seeking God first – together. Three weeks of setting something aside to give him more room.

About the fast

Give God first place again

We often live by a list where God ends up somewhere in the middle. Fasting is a practical way to put him at the top again. You don’t need any experience – we walk through it together, one day at a time.

“But seek first God’s Kingdom and his righteousness; and all these things will be given to you as well.”

Matthew 6:33 · World English Bible
How to join

Three simple steps

1

Start the reading plan

“21 days of prayer and fasting” gives you Bible verses, a short devotional, a practical step and prayer points every day.

Open the reading plan

2

Choose how you will fast

Decide in advance what you will set aside and for how long. A fast you can keep is better than one you give up.

See the suggestions

3

Pray with us

Invite a friend, your family or your small group, and follow His Kingdom Ministry on YouVersion for encouragement along the way.

How to fast

Find a fast that suits you

Fasting is not about performance, but about turning your heart towards God. When you feel hunger or longing, let it remind you to pray.

Partial fast

You skip some meals, such as lunch, or eat simply like Daniel (Dan 1:12) and leave out meat, sweets and coffee.

Fasting from habits

You put away social media, TV, streaming or something else that takes up a lot of time, and spend that time on prayer and God’s Word.

Longer food fast

For those who have fasted before and are in good health: shorter periods with liquids only. Drink enough, and start gently.

Talk to your doctor before fasting from food if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes or another illness, take medication, have or have had a difficult relationship with food, or are under 18. You can fast from habits instead – that fast is just as valuable.

Read the full teaching on fasting

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to fast from food for all 21 days?

No. Many people fast partially or from habits, and some choose a few days of fasting from food. What matters most is that you give God more room.

What if I start later than January 4?

That’s completely fine. You can choose your own start date in the reading plan, and your progress is saved to your profile.

What if I break the fast?

God is gracious. Don’t give up – just start again the next day. Fasting is not an achievement, but an invitation.

Can I get the reading plan on paper?

Yes, there is a printed version. You’ll find it on the reading plan page.

Join us from January 4

We look forward to praying and fasting with you. Feel free to invite someone to join you.
