Structure of the Bible

Explore how the Bible is structured into 66 books across the Old Testament (OT) and New Testament (NT), literary genres, and practical reading strategies.

Back to Media & Resources

The Bible consists of 66 books and is essentially a library written over a period of more than 1,000 years. To make navigation simple, it is divided into the Old Testament (OT) and the New Testament (NT), which are further grouped into distinct literary genres.

Old Testament (39 Books)

Written in Hebrew (and some Aramaic). Covers creation, Israel's history, laws, and prophecies.

Genre 1 5 Books

The Law (Pentateuch / Torah)

Content: Creation, patriarchs (Abraham, Isaac, Jacob), Exodus from Egypt, Ten Commandments, and God's covenant with Israel.

BOOKS INCLUDED:
Genesis Exodus Leviticus Numbers Deuteronomy
Read Pentateuch arrow_forward
Genre 2 12 Books

Historical Books

Content: Israel's settlement, kingdom era (David & Solomon), kingdom division, exile, and restoration.

BOOKS INCLUDED:
Joshua Judges Ruth 1 & 2 Samuel 1 & 2 Kings 1 & 2 Chronicles Ezra Nehemiah Esther
Read Historical Books arrow_forward
Genre 3 5 Books

Wisdom & Poetry

Content: Worship songs, prayers, reflections on justice and suffering, and practical adages.

BOOKS INCLUDED:
Job Psalms Proverbs Ecclesiastes Song of Solomon
Read Psalms & Wisdom arrow_forward
Genre 4 17 Books

The Prophets

Content: Warnings of judgment against injustice, calls to repentance, and Messianic prophecies.

MAJOR PROPHETS:
Isaiah Jeremiah Lamentations Ezekiel Daniel
MINOR PROPHETS:
Hosea Joel Amos Obadiah Jonah Micah Nahum Habakkuk Zephaniah Haggai Zechariah Malachi
Read Prophets arrow_forward

New Testament (27 Books)

Written in Greek. Focuses on Jesus Christ, early Church expansion, and eternal hope.

Genre 1 4 Books

The Gospels

Content: Life, teachings, death, and resurrection of Jesus Christ. Matthew, Mark, and Luke are Synoptic Gospels; John provides deep theological insights.

BOOKS INCLUDED:
Matthew Mark Luke John
Read Gospels arrow_forward
Genre 2 1 Book

Church History

Content: Continuation of Luke's Gospel documenting early Church growth across the Mediterranean.

BOOK INCLUDED:
Acts of the Apostles
Read Acts arrow_forward
Genre 3 21 Books

The Epistles (Letters)

Content: Pauline and General epistles instructing local churches in Christian doctrine and lifestyle.

PAUL'S EPISTLES:
Romans 1 & 2 Corinthians Galatians Ephesians Philippians Colossians 1 & 2 Thessalonians 1 & 2 Timothy Titus Philemon
GENERAL EPISTLES:
Hebrews James 1 & 2 Peter 1, 2, 3 John Jude
Read Epistles arrow_forward
Genre 4 1 Book

Apocalyptic Literature

Content: Symbolic and apocalyptic comfort assuring Christ's ultimate triumph and the new creation.

BOOK INCLUDED:
Revelation

Tip on Interpretation

Always consider the genre you are reading. Poetry (Psalms) should not be interpreted the same way as narrative history (Kings) or practical adages (Proverbs).
