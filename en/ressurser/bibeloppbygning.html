Structure of the Bible & Genres

The Bible is essentially a library written by over 40 authors across three continents over more than 1,000 years. To make navigation easy, it is divided into the Old Testament (OT) and the New Testament (NT), which are further grouped into distinct literary genres.

Old Testament (39 Books)

Written in Hebrew (and some Aramaic). Covers creation, the history of Israel, covenant laws, and prophecies.

Genre 1 5 Books

The Law (Pentateuch / Torah)

Content: Creation, patriarchs (Abraham, Isaac, Jacob), Exodus from Egypt, the Ten Commandments, and God's covenant with Israel.

BOOKS INCLUDED:
Genesis Exodus Leviticus Numbers Deuteronomy
Read Pentateuch arrow_forward
Genre 2 12 Books

Historical Books

Content: Settlement in Promised Land, kingdom golden era (David & Solomon), kingdom division, Babylonian exile, and return.

BOOKS INCLUDED:
Joshua Judges Ruth 1 Samuel 2 Samuel 1 Kings 2 Kings 1 Chronicles 2 Chronicles Ezra Nehemiah Esther
Read Historical Books arrow_forward
Genre 3 5 Books

Wisdom & Poetry

Content: Prayers, worship songs, reflections on suffering and justice, and practical daily wisdom.

BOOKS INCLUDED:
Job Psalms Proverbs Ecclesiastes Song of Solomon

Genre 4 17 Books

The Prophets

Content: Warnings of judgment against injustice, calls to repentance, and promises of the coming Messiah.

MAJOR PROPHETS:
Isaiah Jeremiah Lamentations Ezekiel Daniel
MINOR PROPHETS:
Hosea Joel Amos Obadiah Jonah Micah Nahum Habakkuk Zephaniah Haggai Zechariah Malachi

New Testament (27 Books)

Written in Greek. Focuses on the life, teachings, death, and resurrection of Jesus Christ, the early Church, and Christian hope.

Genre 1 4 Books

The Gospels

Content: Narratives of Jesus' life, teachings, death, and resurrection. Matthew, Mark, and Luke are called the Synoptic Gospels, while John offers a more theological perspective.

BOOKS INCLUDED:
Matthew Mark Luke John
Read Gospels arrow_forward
Genre 2 1 Book

Church History

Content: Continuation of Luke's Gospel. Details the birth and expansion of the early Christian Church across the Roman Empire.

BOOK INCLUDED:
Acts of the Apostles
Read Acts arrow_forward
Genre 3 21 Books

The Epistles (Letters)

Content: Christian doctrine, pastoral guidance for local churches, and personal encouragement.

PAUL'S EPISTLES:
Romans 1 & 2 Corinthians Galatians Ephesians Philippians Colossians 1 & 2 Thessalonians 1 & 2 Timothy Titus Philemon
GENERAL EPISTLES:
Hebrews James 1 & 2 Peter 1, 2, 3 John Jude

Apocalyptic Literature

Content: Symbolic and comforting visions for persecuted Christians, assuring God's ultimate victory over evil and the new creation.

BOOK INCLUDED:
Revelation

How to Read the Bible

If you open the Bible on page 1 and try to read cover-to-cover, most people stop around Leviticus. Here are proven strategies to get started successfully.

1

Start with the Essentials (For Beginners)

Gospel of Mark bolt

The shortest, fast-paced Gospel giving a vivid portrayal of Jesus.

Gospel of John favorite

Great for understanding the profound theological depth of Jesus' words.

Acts of the Apostles groups

Exciting history of how the first Christian movement expanded.

Psalms / Proverbs auto_stories

Read a chapter daily for spiritual encouragement and practical wisdom.

2

Choose a Reading Plan

category

Genre-based

Read a Gospel, then an Epistle (e.g. Philippians), then an OT story (e.g. Joshua). Keeps variety fresh.

schedule

Chronological

Read events in historical timeline sequence.

psychology

Topical

Focus on specific themes such as hope, leadership, or prayer.

info

Tip on Interpretation

Always ask yourself which genre you are reading. Poetry (Psalms) should not be interpreted the same as historical accounts (Kings) or practical adages (Proverbs).
